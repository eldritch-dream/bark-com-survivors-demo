# Bark-Com: Survivors — v0.4 friend demo

**Good Dogs. Dead Gods.**

## What's new

- Meet the Bellwether at 5 minutes and the Threshold Keeper at 10, before the final encounter at 15.
- Bring one active ability: Dash, or Repulsor Pulse after returning alive from a miniboss kill. Upgrade your equipped active during the run. Dash moves you; Repulsor pushes ordinary enemies without dealing damage.
- Find the optional Supply Beacon at 7 minutes. Hold its 3-metre teal circle for 10 cumulative seconds, then collect a 25-second boost to firing speed and pickup reach.
- Exploders now make one warned charge before their normal proximity fuse. Dodge, push them away or kill them to cancel the attack.
- Enjoy the UFO-style Goodboi drone, spooky Bellwether eye, shrine Beacon, refreshed portals, cleaner houses and visible arena fence.
- Clearer pause controls, fullscreen, improved phone layouts, fewer medkits, and XP drops beside medkits instead of hiding inside them.

## Controls

Move with **WASD/arrows** or the phone joystick; weapons fire automatically. **Space/E** or the active button uses your ability. **Escape** pauses; **F** toggles fullscreen where the browser allows it. Phone missions use landscape orientation.

## Saves and feedback

Older saves are supported, and existing missions keep their previous rules. Start a new deployment to see the new mission features. Progress stays in the browser you use. **Save & Quit** preserves a mission; **Retreat** ends it alive with reduced rewards. Combat death is permanent. Newer saves cannot safely be loaded by older builds.

Balance is still evolving, especially Repulsor usefulness and Exploder pressure. Strong builds can still destroy Exploders early. Controller support is not included, and fullscreen/device performance varies by browser.

Tell us your device/browser, mission time, build choices and what happened. Thanks for playing!
